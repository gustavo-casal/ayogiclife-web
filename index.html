About me

Hi, I'm Gustavo.

I was born in Argentina and have lived in Bavaria for more than ten years. Yoga has been with me since childhood. For me it is an art of living, practised on the mat and carried into everyday life.

For over 18 years I have practised tantric yoga, first at the De Rose – Swasthya Yoga school. In 2023 I was certified as a teacher by the Jivamukti Yoga School. I also trained in mindfulness meditation, pranayama and breathwork.

My classes combine movement, conscious breathing and meditation. Group classes are in German; private sessions are also available in English and Spanish.

  • 18+ years of tantric yoga
  • Jivamukti certified, 2023
  • Pranayama & breathwork

The tradition

Body, breath, energy and awareness, practised as one.

Classical tantric yoga sees the body as the doorway, not the obstacle. We work with posture, breath and attention together, so practice reaches deeper than muscles.

The old texts describe this as the meeting of awareness and energy, Shiva and Shakti. In class it simply feels like coming home to yourself.
